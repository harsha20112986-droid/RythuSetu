# RythuSetu — Farmer User Guide (రైతు మార్గదర్శిని)

**Welcome to RythuSetu (రైతుసేతు)!**  
RythuSetu is your all-in-one digital agriculture companion designed for farmers across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. It helps you check authentic market prices, file crop loss claims on time, detect crop diseases with your phone camera, rent farm machinery, find cold storage, and track your farm expenses.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Registration & Login

You can use RythuSetu on any smartphone or computer with internet access.

### 1.1 Creating an Account
1. Open the website: `https://rythu-setu.vercel.app`
2. Click **Sign In** at the top right, then choose **Create Account**.
3. Fill in:
   - Your **Name** and **Mobile Number**
   - Choose a simple **Username** and secure **Password**
   - Select your **State** and **District** (e.g., Warangal, Guntur, Nizamabad)
4. Tap **Register**. You are now registered and logged in!

### 1.2 Signing In Later
- Tap **Sign In**, type your Username and Password, and tap **Login**.
- If you use your personal phone, your login will stay active for easy access.

## 2. Setting Up Your Farm Profile

Setting up your profile helps RythuSetu show you the exact mandi prices, weather warnings, and government schemes for your land:

1. Tap **Profile** in the menu.
2. Enter your farm details:
   - **Village & Mandal**
   - **Landholding Size** (in acres, e.g. 2.5 or 4.0)
   - **Main Crop** (e.g. Cotton, Paddy, Red Chilli, Turmeric, Maize, Groundnut)
   - **Season** (Kharif / Rabi / Summer)
   - **Preferred Language** (Telugu, English, or Hindi)
3. Tap **Save Profile**.

## 3. Finding Mandi Market Prices

The **Mandi Prices** tool helps you decide when and where to sell your harvest for the best return.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["Select Your Crop<br/>(e.g., Cotton)"] --> B["Select Your District<br/>(e.g., Warangal)"]
    B --> C["Check Modal Price vs MSP"]
    C --> D["Choose Sell Now or Store"]
```

### 3.1 Understanding the Price Terms
- **Modal Price (సగటు మోడల్ ధర):** This is the **most common price** at which the highest volume of crop traded in the market that day. It is more realistic than the absolute highest or lowest price.
- **Minimum Support Price - MSP (కనీస మద్దతు ధర):** The official benchmark price announced by the Government of India (CACP) before the sowing season.
- **The Difference:**
  - *APMC Mandi Price* changes every day based on daily arrivals and buyer demand.
  - *MSP* is the fixed statutory reference floor set by the government for Fair Average Quality (FAQ) produce.
  - **Rule of Thumb:** If the market modal price is below MSP, consider storing your harvest in a registered cold storage or approaching government procurement centers.

### 3.2 Understanding Freshness Badges
Every price displays a transparency tag so you know how fresh the numbers are:
- 🟢 **Latest (ఈరోజు ధర):** Fresh data from today's active APMC market session.
- 🟡 **Recent (నిన్నటి ధర):** Reported yesterday or within the last 48 hours.
- 🟠 **Delayed (ఆలస్యమైన సమాచారం):** 3 to 5 days old (typically due to weekend yard holidays).
- ⚪ **Stale (పాత ధర):** Older than 5 days. Use only as historical reference.

## 4. Weather Forecast & Harvest Shield

- Tap **Weather** to view live temperature, humidity, rainfall probabilities, and 7-day outlooks for your mandal.
- **Harvest Shield:** If your crop is nearing harvest, enter how many days are left until cutting. Harvest Shield will tell you if the upcoming 72 hours are safe for threshing and grain drying, or if unexpected storm clouds mean you should cover your harvest.

## 5. Finding Government Schemes

- Tap **Government Schemes** to see Central and State welfare programs you qualify for:
  - **PM-KISAN:** ₹6,000 direct income support per year in 3 installments.
  - **Telangana Rythu Bharosa:** Direct financial assistance per acre per season.
  - **PMFBY:** Subsidized crop insurance for natural disaster protection.
  - **SMAM:** Subsidies up to 50% on tractors, seed drills, and agricultural machinery.
  - **e-NWR Storage Loan:** Pledge loan up to 75% of your stored crop's value so you do not have to sell in distress.
- Tap **Estimate Benefits** to see the total annual rupee support your family can claim.

## 6. Using Crop Doctor (AI Leaf Disease Diagnosis)

When you notice spots, yellowing, or curling on your leaves:

1. Tap **Crop Doctor** in the menu.
2. Tap **Take Photo** or upload a clear picture of the affected leaf in daylight.
3. Select the crop (e.g. Cotton, Chilli, Paddy).
4. Tap **Diagnose Disease**.

### What the Results Mean
- **Detected Issue:** Shows the common and scientific name of the pest or fungus (e.g., *Bacterial Leaf Blight* or *Chilli Thrips*).
- **Recommended Remedy:** Step-by-step guidance on approved sprays, dosage (e.g., grams per liter of water), and organic practices.
- **When to Consult an Officer:** If the disease is severe or widespread across several acres, the app will advise you to visit your Mandal Agriculture Officer (MAO) immediately for certified prescription.

## 7. Krishi AI Assistant (మీ వ్యవసాయ సహాయకుడు)

Tap **Krishi Assistant** to ask farming questions in your own language (Telugu, Hindi, or English). You can type or tap the microphone icon to speak.

### What Krishi Assistant CAN Do:
- Check current mandi rates for your district.
- Explain pest prevention steps for your crop.
- Tell you the required documents for PM-KISAN or PMFBY.
- Calculate seed rate or fertilizer (Urea, DAP, Potash) dosage for your acreage.

### What Krishi Assistant CANNOT Do:
- It cannot approve or disburse insurance claims directly.
- It cannot guarantee commercial market prices.
- It is an advisory tool; always verify critical chemical treatments with your local agricultural officer.

## 8. Filing a PMFBY Crop Loss Claim (72-Hour Window)

If extreme weather (hailstorm, flood, unseasonal storm, or drought) damages your standing crop:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The 72-Hour Rule:** Under official PMFBY rules, you **MUST file your loss report within 72 hours (3 days)** of the calamity occurring. Reports submitted after 72 hours face severe rejection risks by insurance companies.

### How to File on RythuSetu:
1. Tap **Crop Loss Claim** in the menu.
2. Select your crop, date of damage, and the cause (Flood, Cyclone, Hailstorm, Pest).
3. Enter the affected acreage and approximate damage percentage (e.g., 50%, 75%).
4. **Take 2–3 clear photos** showing the damaged field and lodged crops.
5. Tap **Submit Intimation**.
6. You will receive an official reference number (e.g., `RYTHU-CLAIM-2026-1044`).
7. Save this number! You can track the joint survey and settlement status anytime in the app under **Track Claim**.

## 9. Agri Khata (రైతు ఖాతా - Farm Expense Ledger)

Keep track of your farming investments to know your true profit:
1. Tap **Agri Khata**.
2. Select your crop and acreage.
3. Enter what you spent on:
   - Seeds
   - Land preparation & plowing
   - Fertilizers & manure
   - Pest control sprays
   - Harvesting & labor
4. Enter your expected yield in quintals.
5. **Breakeven Calculation:** The ledger calculates your exact **Cost of Production per Quintal**. If your cost is ₹4,200/quintal, you know you should not sell below this rate in the mandi!

## 10. Renting Machinery & Booking Cold Storage

### Custom Hiring Center (CHC) Machinery
- Tap **Machinery Rentals** to find tractors, paddy combine harvesters, rotavators, and agriculture spraying drones near your village.
- View hourly or per-acre rates, owner phone numbers, and book equipment directly.

### Cold Storage & Warehouses
- Tap **Cold Storage** to find WDRA-regulated cold storages in Guntur, Warangal, Khammam, and Kurnool.
- View monthly rent per bag, remaining capacity, and check if the warehouse offers **e-NWR pledge loans** (so you can borrow money from the bank while your crop waits for higher market prices).
